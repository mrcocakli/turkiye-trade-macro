What changed this month

Ranked by how unusual each move is against that series' own last 24 months, so a quiet series that jumps outranks a volatile one doing what it always does. The text is generated from the database by rule.

    Coverage and method

    Every series the pipeline is configured to pull, and what the last run returned.

    • Sources: TCMB EVDS (exchange rates, interest rates, prices, trade) and World Bank Open Data (annual context). No paid or private data.
    • Daily series are shown at the last observation of each month; the date under each figure says which day that is.
    • Changes in rates and ratios are in percentage points; changes in levels are in per cent.
    • "Typical move" is the median absolute monthly change over the previous 24 months.
    • This is a monitoring page built on public data. It is not a forecast or investment advice.
